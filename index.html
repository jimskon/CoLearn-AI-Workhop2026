Overview

What This Workshop Is About

Large Language Models make it possible for students to generate correct answers and working code while engaging minimally with the reasoning those outputs are intended to demonstrate. We call this cognitive bypass — and it presents a real challenge for computing educators.

This workshop introduces coLearn-AI, a platform that structures small-group collaboration so that AI scaffolds student reasoning rather than replacing it. You will first experience the platform as a participant, then learn to author activities for your own courses.

🧠
Explain cognitive bypass and why it matters for CS assessment.
🤝
Describe POGIL-style roles and how they distribute responsibility.
✍️
Design a working activity with feedback prompts and sample responses.
🚀
Plan deployment of coLearn-AI in your own classroom.
🔬
Identify research opportunities and collaborations.
Agenda 3 hours total

Workshop Schedule

  • 0:00 25 min
    Part 1 — Introduction & System Overview Cognitive bypass, Piaget & Vygotsky, POGIL roles, the four guidance layers, and a live demo of the platform.
  • 0:25 35 min
    Part 2 — Hands-on Activity Experience Work in small groups through a real coLearn-AI activity (For Loops in Python). Experience the student side — roles, turn-taking, AI nudges, and the epistemic trace.
  • 1:00 10 min
    ☕ Break Stretch, grab coffee. When you return you'll switch from participant to author.
  • 1:10 60 min
    Part 3 — Activity Design & Implementation Learn the authoring format, write feedback prompts and sample responses for 2–3 questions, and test your activity live in the platform's authoring tools.
  • 2:10 30 min
    Part 4 — Deployment & Classroom Integration Group formation, progress monitoring, reviewing the epistemic trace, hosting options, and adapting existing materials.
  • 2:40 20 min
    Part 5 — Research Collaboration & Discussion Lessons learned, open design tensions, research questions, participant questions, and how to get involved.
Before You Start

What You Need

💻 A Laptop

With a modern browser (Chrome or Firefox work best) and WiFi. No additional software installation is needed.

🔗 Platform Access

The workshop facilitator will provide login credentials for colearn-ai.com. You do not need to create an account in advance.

🐍 Some Python Familiarity

Part 2 uses a Python for-loop activity. CS1 comfort is sufficient — you do not need to be an expert.

Audience Computing educators — especially instructors of introductory programming or data structures courses — who want to incorporate AI into their teaching while preserving meaningful student engagement. No prior experience with AI tools or activity-authoring platforms is required.
