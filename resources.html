CCSC:MW 2026 · Kenyon College · Oct 9–10, 2026

AI-Mediated Collaborative Cognition: Designing Systems That Preserve Reasoning in the Age of LLMs

James Paul Skon

When LLMs can produce correct answers with little effort, students risk cognitive bypass: correct results without the reasoning those results are supposed to reflect. The paper argues for system-level design that makes reasoning a condition for progress. It presents coLearn-AI as an example of that approach, with role-based collaboration, turn-taking, AI limited to a scaffolding role, and an append-only interaction trace.

View PDF
CCSC:NE · April 11, 2026

AI-Enhanced Guided-Inquiry Platform for Collaborative Learning in Introductory Programming

Bo Kim, James Skon

Introduces coLearn-AI, which combines guided-inquiry pedagogy with AI formative feedback for group work in introductory programming. Instructors author structured activities and manage groups, and they embed AI guidance directives in activity files to keep feedback aligned with their teaching goals. The platform includes synchronous workspaces, turn-taking, and analytics dashboards.

View PDF
ASEE Northeast Section · March 26, 2026

Automated Assessment using AI-Enhanced Guided-Inquiry Learning Platform

Bo Kim, James Skon

Describes an assessment mode for the platform. In this mode it delivers work to one learner at a time, turns off formative AI help, and runs submissions through an AI first-pass grade constrained by instructor rubrics, with each score explained by reference to the student's work. Instructors review the AI's grades, adjust them, and publish final scores through an auditable interface.
